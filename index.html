How targets are calculated

Calories use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation for estimated BMR multiplied by the selected activity factor. Nutrient targets use general adult Dietary Reference Intake-style targets by sex and age. This app is for personal tracking and education, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
